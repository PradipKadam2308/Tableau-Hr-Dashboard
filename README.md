
# HR Analytics Dashboard - Employee Retention & Attrition

## 📊 Project Overview

This project features an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Tableau. The primary goal is to provide HR departments with a clear overview of workforce health, focusing specifically on **Employee Attrition**. By analyzing demographics, job satisfaction, and educational backgrounds, this dashboard helps stakeholders identify high-risk groups and implement better retention strategies.



## 🔑 Key Metrics (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks several critical High-Level Metrics:

  * **Total Employee Count:** 1,470
  * **Attrition Count:** 237 (Employees who left the company)
  * **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
  * **Active Employees:** 1,233
  * **Average Age:** 37 years

-----

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Insights

### 1\. Departmental Analysis

  * **R\&D** carries the highest attrition volume (56.12%), followed by **Sales** (38.82%).
  * The **HR Department** has the lowest attrition at 5.06%.

### 2\. Demographic Breakdown

  * **Attrition by Gender:** Men leave at a significantly higher rate (150) compared to women (87).
  * **Age Group Impact:** The **25-34 age bracket** sees the highest attrition (112 employees), suggesting that mid-career professionals are the most likely to seek new opportunities.
  * **Education Field:** Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** backgrounds represent the majority of the attrition count.

### 3\. Job Satisfaction & Retention

  * The dashboard includes a **Heatmap** cross-referencing Job Roles with Satisfaction Ratings (1–4).
  * **Sales Executives** and **Research Scientists** show higher volumes of employees in the "Satisfied" (4) category, yet still show significant attrition, suggesting factors other than satisfaction (like pay or growth) may be at play.

-----

## 🛠️ Tools Used

  * **Tableau Desktop:** For data visualization and dashboard design.
  * **Data Source:** (e.g., CSV/Excel file containing employee demographics and employment status).
  * **Data Cleaning:** Performed within Tableau (Data Interpreter/Calculated Fields).
